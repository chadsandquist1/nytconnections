10:00 AM - Turkey Start

  • Preheat main oven to 325°F
  • Remove turkey from packaging and pat completely dry with paper towels
  • Place turkey breast-side up in roasting pan with rack
  • Tent loosely with aluminum foil
  • Place in oven - will cook for approximately 7 hours
  • Set a reminder to check temperature at 4:00 PM

Note: For a 15 lb turkey, allow approximately 28 minutes per pound. Target internal temperature is 165°F in the thickest part of the thigh.

10:15 AM - Prep Mise En Place

  • Chop 4 cups onions (about 2 large onions)
  • Chop 3 cups celery (about 6-8 stalks)
  • Mince 3-4 cloves garlic
  • Get all ingredients organized on counter
  • Set out all baking dishes you'll need (three 9x13 pans)
  • Grease baking dishes with butter or cooking spray

Tip: This prep work will save you time later. Store prepped vegetables in bowls covered with damp paper towels.

12:30 PM - Sweet Potato Casserole

  • Peel and cube 4 lbs sweet potatoes
  • Place in large pot, cover with water, bring to boil
  • Boil 20 minutes until fork-tender
  • Drain thoroughly and return to pot
  • Mash with: 1/2 cup butter, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 2 eggs (beaten), 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Transfer to greased 9x13 pan
  • Make topping: Mix 1 cup brown sugar, 1/2 cup flour, 1/3 cup melted butter, 1 cup chopped pecans
  • Sprinkle topping evenly over sweet potato mixture
  • Cover with foil and refrigerate

Will bake at 4:00 PM for 30 minutes at 350°F

1:00 PM - Homemade Stuffing

  • Cube 1 large loaf of bread into 1-inch pieces (12-16 cups)
  • Spread on baking sheets and toast in 250°F toaster oven for 15 minutes until dried out
  • In large skillet, melt 1/2 cup butter over medium heat
  • Add 2 cups diced onions and 2 cups diced celery
  • Sauté 8-10 minutes until softened
  • Add 2 tsp dried sage, 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • In large bowl, combine toasted bread cubes and vegetable mixture
  • Gradually add 2-3 cups chicken broth until moist but not soggy
  • Transfer to greased 9x13 pan, cover with foil, refrigerate

Will bake at 4:00 PM for 30 minutes at 350°F

1:45 PM - Stovetop Stuffing

  • Prepare 2 boxes of Stovetop Stuffing according to package directions
  • Typically: Bring specified amount of water and butter to boil
  • Remove from heat, stir in stuffing mix
  • Let stand 5 minutes
  • Transfer to oven-safe dish
  • Cover with foil and set aside

Will reheat at 4:15 PM for 25 minutes at 350°F

2:00 PM - Green Bean Casserole

  • Fresh beans: Trim 2 lbs green beans, blanch in boiling water 5 minutes, drain
  • OR canned: Drain 4 cans (15 oz each) green beans
  • In large bowl, mix beans with:
  • 2 cans (10.5 oz each) cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Transfer to greased 9x13 pan
  • Top with 1.5 cups french fried onions
  • Cover with foil and refrigerate

Will bake at 4:15 PM for 25 minutes at 350°F

2:30 PM - Cranberry Sauce

  • In medium saucepan, combine:
  • 12 oz fresh cranberries (1 bag)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • Bring to boil over medium-high heat
  • Reduce to simmer
  • Cook 10-15 minutes until berries pop and sauce thickens
  • Remove from heat and let cool
  • Transfer to serving bowl and refrigerate

Serve cold or at room temperature

3:00 PM - Mashed Potatoes

  • Peel and cube 5 lbs potatoes (Russet or Yukon Gold work best)
  • Place in large pot and cover with cold water
  • Add 1 tablespoon salt to water
  • Bring to boil, then reduce to simmer
  • Cook 15-20 minutes until fork-tender
  • Drain thoroughly and return to pot
  • Mash with potato masher or use mixer
  • Add: 1/2 cup butter (cubed), 1 cup warm milk or cream
  • Mix until smooth and creamy
  • Season with salt and pepper to taste
  • Transfer to oven-safe serving dish, cover with foil

Will reheat at 4:15 PM for 25 minutes at 350°F

3:45 PM - Shape Bread

  • Remove risen dough from refrigerator
  • Shape into dinner rolls or a loaf as desired
  • Place on greased baking sheet or in loaf pan
  • Let rest at room temperature for 15 minutes
  • Set aside until 4:15 PM

Will bake in toaster oven at 4:15 PM

4:00 PM - Oven Coordination (CRITICAL TIME!)

  • Check turkey temperature - insert thermometer in thickest part of thigh
  • If 165°F or higher: Remove turkey from oven!
  • Tent turkey with foil and let rest (20-30 minutes minimum)
  • Increase main oven temperature to 350°F
  • Preheat second oven to 350°F
  • Place in ovens:
  • Main oven: Sweet potato casserole (uncovered, 30 min)
  • Second oven: Homemade stuffing (uncovered, 30 min)

Note: If turkey isn't quite ready, wait 15 minutes and check again. Adjust remaining timeline accordingly.

4:15 PM - Final Baking Push

  • Add to available oven space (both ovens at 350°F):
  • Green bean casserole (uncovered, 25 min)
  • Stovetop stuffing (covered, 25 min)
  • Mashed potatoes (covered with foil, 25 min)
  • Toaster oven/Air Fryer:
  • Bread rolls or loaf (375°F, 20-25 minutes until golden)

Set timer for 25 minutes! Everything will finish at 4:40 PM

4:30 PM - Make Gravy

  • Pour turkey drippings from roasting pan into fat separator or measuring cup
  • Let fat rise to top, skim off excess (or use as is for richer gravy)
  • In saucepan, melt 1/4 cup butter
  • Whisk in 1/4 cup flour to make roux
  • Cook 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until golden
  • Gradually whisk in 3 cups liquid (drippings + chicken broth to make 3 cups)
  • Bring to simmer, whisking constantly
  • Simmer 5-7 minutes until thickened
  • Season with salt and pepper to taste
  • Transfer to gravy boat or serving bowl

Keep warm on low heat or in microwave until serving

4:45 PM - Final Assembly

  • Remove all dishes from ovens
  • Remove foil from covered dishes
  • Check bread - should be golden brown
  • Carve turkey:
  • Remove legs and thighs
  • Slice breast meat
  • Arrange on serving platter
  • Transfer all side dishes to serving bowls/platters
  • Place cranberry sauce in serving bowl
  • Put butter on table for bread
  • Do final check - everything ready to go!

5:00 PM - SERVE & ENJOY! 🦃

  • Set out all dishes buffet-style or place on table
  • Let guests serve themselves
  • Enjoy your perfectly timed Thanksgiving feast!
  • Remember: dishes will stay warm for 15-20 minutes

🎉 Happy Thanksgiving! 🎉

📖 Complete Recipe Details

🦃 TURKEY (15 lbs, Pre-Seasoned)

Cooking Time: 7 hours at 325°F (approximately 28 minutes per pound)

Done When: Internal temperature reaches 165°F in thickest part of thigh

Resting: Let rest 20-30 minutes before carving for juiciest results

🥔 MASHED POTATOES

  • 5 lbs potatoes (Russet or Yukon Gold), peeled and cubed
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup warm milk or cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions: Boil potatoes until tender (15-20 min), drain, mash with butter and milk until smooth, season to taste.

🥬 GREEN BEAN CASSEROLE

  • 2 lbs fresh green beans (or 4 15-oz cans, drained)
  • 2 10.5-oz cans cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1.5 cups french fried onions
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions: Mix all ingredients except onions, transfer to 9x13 pan, top with onions. Bake 25 min at 350°F.

🍠 SWEET POTATO CASSEROLE

Base:

  • 4 lbs sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Topping:

  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1 cup chopped pecans

Instructions: Boil sweet potatoes until tender, mash with butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla. Spread in 9x13 pan. Mix topping ingredients, sprinkle over sweet potatoes. Bake 30 min at 350°F.

🍞 HOMEMADE STUFFING

  • 1 large loaf bread (12-16 cups cubed)
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 cups diced onions
  • 2 cups diced celery
  • 2 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2-3 cups chicken broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions: Toast bread cubes at 250°F for 15 min. Sauté onions and celery in butter until soft, add herbs. Combine with bread cubes and broth. Bake 30 min at 350°F.

📦 STOVETOP STUFFING

  • 2 boxes Stovetop Stuffing Mix
  • Follow package directions for water and butter

Instructions: Prepare according to package directions. Transfer to oven-safe dish, cover and reheat at 350°F for 25 minutes.

🍒 CRANBERRY SAUCE

  • 12 oz fresh cranberries (1 bag)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water

Instructions: Combine all ingredients in saucepan. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer. Cook 10-15 minutes until berries pop and sauce thickens. Cool and refrigerate.

🍖 TURKEY GRAVY

  • Turkey drippings from roasting pan
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups liquid (drippings + chicken broth)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions: Melt butter, whisk in flour to make roux. Gradually add liquid, whisking constantly. Simmer 5-7 minutes until thickened. Season to taste.

🥖 BREAD

Your prepared, risen dough

Instructions: Shape into rolls or loaf, let rest 15 minutes. Bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until golden brown.

  • Large pot for potatoes

  • Saucepan for gravy

Tools Needed

  • Whisk
  • Wooden spoons
  • Sharp knife & cutting board
  • Measuring cups & spoons
  • Colander
  • Meat thermometer (essential!)
  • Aluminum foil
  • Serving dishes & utensils

🔥 Oven Strategy

10:00 AM - 4:00 PM: Main Oven Only

325°F - Turkey cooking alone

4:00 PM - 4:30 PM: Both Ovens at 350°F

Main Oven: Sweet potato casserole (30 min)

Second Oven: Homemade stuffing (30 min)

4:15 PM - 4:40 PM: Full Oven Utilization

Add to available space in both ovens:

  • Green bean casserole (25 min)
  • Stovetop stuffing (25 min)
  • Mashed potatoes (25 min)

Toaster Oven: Bread (375°F, 20-25 min)

Emergency Backup

Microwave: Use only for quick reheating if needed

💡 Tips for Success

  • ✅ Start turkey on time - it's your anchor dish and takes the longest
  • ✅ Use the 6+ hours of turkey cooking time to prep all vegetables and casseroles
  • ✅ Keep casseroles covered with foil until final baking to retain heat
  • ✅ Check turkey temperature at 4:00 PM - better to have extra resting time
  • ✅ Label covered dishes with sticky notes so you know what's what
  • ✅ Have all serving dishes ready and on counter before final 30 minutes
  • ✅ Make gravy while other dishes are finishing in ovens
  • ✅ If ovens are full, mashed potatoes hold heat well when covered
  • ✅ Everything can sit covered for 10-15 minutes without getting cold
  • ✅ Don't stress if timing shifts by 15 minutes - food is forgiving!
